Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 8, 2026

Introduction

Price Compare ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected and used when you use our mobile application.

In Short: Price Compare does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from users. All data stays on your device. However, we use third-party services (Google AdMob) that may collect certain information.

Information We Collect

Price Compare does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information from users. The app works entirely offline, and all price comparison data stays on your device.

Data Stored Locally (On Your Device Only)

  • Price comparison data: Product names, prices, quantities, and units you enter
  • App preferences: Language selection (Thai/English), tutorial completion status
  • Camera images: Processed locally for OCR scanning and NOT saved or transmitted

Important: None of this data ever leaves your device. We cannot access it.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party service that may collect information:

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process the following data:

  • Advertising ID (AAID): A unique identifier for your device used for advertising purposes
  • IP address: Your device's internet protocol address
  • Device information: Model, operating system version, screen size
  • Ad interaction data: Clicks, impressions, and viewing time

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Camera Permission

The app requests camera permission for the following purposes:

  • Scanning product prices using OCR (Optical Character Recognition)
  • Scanning product quantities and units

Camera images are processed locally on your device using Google ML Kit Text Recognition and are NOT uploaded, stored, or transmitted anywhere. Images are discarded immediately after processing.

Data Storage and Security

All price comparison data (products, prices, quantities) is stored locally on your device only using Android's SharedPreferences. We do not have servers and cannot access your data.

Your data remains on your device until you:

  • Clear app data manually from device settings
  • Uninstall the application

Permissions Requested

The app requests the following permissions:

  • CAMERA: To scan prices and quantities using OCR
  • INTERNET: To display Google AdMob advertisements

Children's Privacy

Price Compare does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. The app is safe for all ages as we do not collect any personal information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no data to access, modify, or delete from our servers. All data is stored locally on your device and you have full control over it through your device settings.
